# Level 01 polish pass 2

This pass is intentionally limited to the first campaign level. It removes the remaining editor-like chrome visible in the iPad review screenshot and unifies the actual play objects into one modern visual language.

Key goals:
- one clean top bar instead of floating duplicate HUD/task controls;
- no signal editor or technical controls in Level 01;
- one consistent rail material for authored and player rails;
- invisible safety walls and a subtle floor boundary;
- a cleaner target ball, bonus markers and receiver;
- a near-blank play surface with soft start/finish ambience;
- inventory collapses once all three rails are placed;
- existing physics, solvability and campaign behavior remain unchanged.
